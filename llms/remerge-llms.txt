# Remerge

> Remerge is a mobile demand-side platform (DSP) specializing in app retargeting, re-engagement, and user acquisition. It is a managed advertising service (no public developer API); campaigns are run through the Remerge platform with support from its account teams.

## Company
- [Remerge](https://www.remerge.io): App retargeting and user-acquisition DSP for gaming, e-commerce, and delivery apps.
- [Help Center](https://help.remerge.io/hc/en-us): Support and product knowledge base.
- [Blog](https://www.remerge.io/blog-post): The latest news in mobile advertising.

## Trust & Security
- [Trust Center](https://trust.remerge.io/): ISO/IEC 27001:2022 ISMS, GDPR, CCPA, CPRA, annual third-party penetration testing (SafeBase by Drata).
- [Vulnerability Disclosure Policy](https://www.remerge.io/remerge-vulnerability-disclosure-policy): Responsible disclosure; contact security@remerge.io (no paid bug bounty).
